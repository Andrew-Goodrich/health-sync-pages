Personal Health Sync

Personal Health Sync is a single-user tool. It copies its owner's own Google Health data (sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, steps and exercise) read-only into the owner's own Google Drive, once a day, for personal analysis.

It has one user, its owner, and is not offered to the public.
