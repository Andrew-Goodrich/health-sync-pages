Personal Health Sync - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

What the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app reads, through the Google Health API and with read-only scopes, the owner's own sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, step and exercise data.

Why

The data is copied once a day into files in the owner's own Google Drive so that the owner can analyze it later. It is not used for any other purpose.

Where the data goes

Data is stored only in the owner's own Google Drive. The app runs inside the owner's own Google account (Google Apps Script). The data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any other person or service, and is not used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the files in Google Drive removes the stored copies.
